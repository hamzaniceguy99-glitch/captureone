What we provide

Capture One provides the operation of tourist accommodation by CAPTURE ONE, a company incorporated under Belgian law and registered with the Crossroads Bank for Enterprises under enterprise number 1007.807.135. CAPACITY IN WHICH THE COMPANY ACTS: the company contracts with guests in its own name, as the operator of the accommodation, whether the accommodation is owned by it or leased by it from a third party. It does not act as an agent, intermediary or manager on behalf of a property owner. The management, letting or negotiation of property belonging to another person, for reward and on a habitual basis, is a regulated activity in Belgium under the Law of 11 February 2013 and its implementing Royal Decree, reserved to persons registered with the Professional Institute of Estate Agents, and the titles of estate agent intermediary and property manager are protected. The company is not registered with that institute, does not use those titles, does not hold client funds and does not carry out transactions on behalf of owners. Where an owner wishes their property to be operated as tourist accommodation, the arrangement takes the form of a lease to the company, under which the owner receives rent and the company becomes the operator and bears the obligations of an operator. REGISTRATION AND FIRE SAFETY: tourist accommodation in Flanders is governed by the Decree of 5 February 2016 on tourist accommodation. Each unit is notified to Toerisme Vlaanderen before being offered, complies with the basic requirements that decree lays down, and holds a fire safety certificate issued by the mayor of the municipality concerned. Registration references and certificate references for each unit: [NUMÉROS D’ENREGISTREMENT ET RÉFÉRENCES DES ATTESTATIONS PAR UNITÉ]. SHORT-TERM RENTAL REGISTRATION NUMBER: under Regulation (EU) 2024/1028 on the collection and sharing of data relating to short-term accommodation rental services, applicable since 20 May 2026, the unique registration number of each unit is stated in every listing, including on online platforms, which are required to verify it. CO-OWNERSHIP: where a unit forms part of a building in co-ownership, short-term letting may be restricted or prohibited by the deed of co-ownership, the co-ownership rules or a decision of the general meeting, and such a restriction is enforceable. The position is verified for each unit before it is offered, and an owner proposing to lease a flat to the company is asked to produce those documents. TOURIST TAX: the municipal tourist tax applicable to the stay is payable and is stated separately from the accommodation price. PRICES AND PAYMENT: the price shown for a direct booking is the total payable for the accommodation, with the tourist tax shown separately. For direct bookings the balance is payable on arrival rather than in advance. Bookings made through an online platform are subject to that platform's own payment and cancellation terms, which prevail over these. NO WARRANTY AS TO THIRD PARTIES: no warranty is given in respect of transport, weather, building works in the vicinity or any service supplied by a third party. The company does not provide legal advice, which is reserved to the Bar, nor accountancy or tax services, which are reserved to professionals registered with the ITAA. Services are described on the Services page and indicative prices on the home page. No physical goods are sold or shipped through this site.
